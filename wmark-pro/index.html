Features

Everything the app does today.

Watermarks

  • Your logo, from an image file or a link, flipping in 3D at intervals.
  • Warning text over the video, all the time or at the times you set, in as many layers as you need.
  • Canary frames: brief marks at keyed moments, different for every video.With the purchase
  • Random text at keyed times and places, anywhere in the picture or among the spots you choose.With the purchase
  • Copyright and creator details written into every copy, and a frame of your choice as the MP4's thumbnail.

Working with it

  • Drop a batch of videos on the window: each becomes a render in the queue, with progress, Cancel and Show in Explorer.
  • A live preview draws the watermark on your own video, or on a sample clip, at the moment you choose.
  • Profiles keep your settings under a name, the app remembers your last run, and each setting shows where its value comes from.
  • The Guided view shows only what you're working on; the Advanced view shows every setting.
  • Choose the codec, quality and sound. Render with the FFmpeg included, or with your own.
  • 18 languages, light and dark, a colour of your choice, and three styles: Standard, Windows and Premium.Premium with the purchase

How it works

  1. Add your videos

    Your logo, warning text and, with the purchase, canary frames and random text. Keep them as a profile for next time.

  3. Render the batch

    Each video renders on your PC, one after another, with timing keyed to it.

What watermarks can and can't do

Visible marks raise the cost of removing them; no watermark makes removal impossible. Canary frames help show that a copy came from you; they don't stop anyone from copying.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Wmark Pro: Video Watermarker

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Core Principle: Local Processing First

EchoJustus ("I", "me", or "my") operates the Wmark Pro: Video Watermarker desktop application (the "Software"). The Software is designed strictly as a privacy-first, offline-capable desktop creator workbench. All core functionalities—including video rendering, text/image watermarking, and Canary Frame (forensic watermark) injection, keyed with a secret that is created and kept on your device—are executed entirely locally on your device. I do not upload, collect, monitor, or have access to your video files, media assets, export histories, or cryptographic private keys. Your creative assets belong to you and never leave your machine through my Software.

2. Information I Collect and How I Use It

Because the Software operates locally, I only interact with the absolute minimum data necessary for software licensing. If you download or purchase the Software via the Microsoft Store, your transaction, payment details, and Microsoft account information are securely handled by Microsoft. To verify your purchase and unlock Pro features, the Software asks the Microsoft Store on your device, through Microsoft's StoreContext API, whether your Microsoft account owns the unlock; that answer stays on your device, and I do not receive it. If you are using a version activated with a license key (a direct download rather than the Microsoft Store), the Software only verifies the mathematical validity of your license key locally, without contacting any server. Your custom preferences, such as custom FFmpeg paths and theme colors, are saved locally on your computer's hard drive and are never transmitted to any external servers.

3. Declaration of Non-Applicability (General Telemetry and Cloud Data)

Many modern software services and storefront platforms collect extensive personal data, including demographic details, browsing history, voice clips, biometric data, location data, and bot usage interactions. I want to explicitly declare that Wmark Pro: Video Watermarker does not engage in any of these practices. The Software does not track your behavior, serve personalized advertising, or collect telemetry data for product improvement. Any broad data collection policies typical of cloud services or large-scale operating systems are fundamentally inapplicable to the internal operation of the Wmark Pro: Video Watermarker application.

4. Third-Party Services and Components

The Software utilizes the open-source FFmpeg engine for media processing. Whether you use the locally bundled version or configure a custom binary path in the settings, the engine runs completely offline and does not transmit any media data over the internet. If you choose to add a logo from a web link, the Software downloads that one image from the address you enter, and that website receives the request as it would from any browser; nothing else is sent. For users of the Microsoft Store version, the official Microsoft Privacy Statement applies exclusively to your interactions with the storefront and in-app purchase systems, not to the Software itself.

5. Children's Privacy

My Software does not address anyone under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under this age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
